# Round-2 regeneration scope

The evidence matrix distinguishes these routes:

- Core, frontier and final causal specificity: archived immutable Round-1 baseline states through full Round-2 continuations, aggregation and inference. It does not claim all Round-1 state evolution was rerun from initial seeds in this gate.
- Stage A/B screening, Stage C/D and independent landscapes: initial deterministic seeds through evolution and Round-2 intervention analysis. The final Stage-D design selection is a frozen experimental input.
- Expected and realized marginal diagnostics: deterministic regeneration from archived Round-1 states.
- Reader records: numerical projection of regenerated scientific tables with frozen display-label templates.

All 29 current source CSVs are accounted for, including historical reader-record aliases. Newly generated raw continuation tables, full control viability levels and 256 regenerated landscape population states are retained. Frozen source tables remain reference targets; the stale compact empirical diagnostic record is retained historically and corrected in the active source set.

The original source of the MP module is reconstructed, not recovered byte-for-byte. Gate-8 records and original recovery reports are preserved for traceability. Manuscript and Supplement files are excluded; historical numerical audits are retained separately from publication assets.

The current scope is a code-and-data scientific regeneration release with numerical reporting alignment. Publishing, tagging, DOI creation and submission are separate actions.
